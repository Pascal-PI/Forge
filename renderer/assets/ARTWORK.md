# Summit artwork

`summit-hero.png` is an original 2172 × 724 image generated for FORGE using the built-in image-generation tool on October 26, 2026. It is bundled locally; the app does not fetch artwork from an external service.

Prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide cinematic landscape artwork for the hero banner of a dark learning dashboard. Primary request: an original majestic indigo alpine mountain range under a deep starry dusk sky, with a small luminous golden winding trail climbing a dramatic summit and a tiny glowing flag at its peak. Beautiful atmospheric depth, refined painterly 3D illustration, finely detailed faceted rock, subtle teal and violet highlights, warm gold only around trail and summit. Composition: ultra-wide landscape approximately 3:1, summit centered at roughly 68 percent width; darkest uncluttered navy negative space across left 40 percent for UI copy, distant low silhouettes there; foreground ridge along bottom; top margin spacious dark sky. Restrained glow, premium calm aspirational mood. No text, no lettering, no UI, no logos, no humans, no watermark, no border. Fully opaque background.

## Spark Lab characters and figures (1.12.0)

Ember (gold flame), Sprig (teal seedling), and Bit (violet robot) are original SVG emoji characters authored in `renderer/spark-lab.js`. Their expressions reflect the current challenge state. The same file draws the responsive balance, staircase tiles, and logic circuits directly from the learner’s settings. These vectors contain no external assets, copied artwork, fonts, or network requests. Theme styles are in `renderer/spark-lab.css`.
